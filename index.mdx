Planetary Talent is a hiring accelerator. You describe the role, we run the search, evaluate 1,000 or more humans, interview the strongest, and deliver a shortlist of three to five people who are ready to hire. Your time per hire is about three hours. It is free to start, and a flat fee is owed only when you hire.

## Who this is for

Planetary Talent fills full-time individual contributor roles for founder-led companies of roughly 5 to 25 employees. Engineering, marketing, finance, design, operations. It is a managed service, not hiring software: you review a shortlist and interview finalists, and we do everything else.

It is not the right fit for free job listings, hourly or shift staffing, short-term or fractional work, or executive search. See [when to use Planetary Talent](/how-it-works/search-tiers#when-planetary-talent-is-the-right-fit).
